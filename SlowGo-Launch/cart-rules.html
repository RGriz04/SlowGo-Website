Golf cart rules by town

Golf cart rules change from town to town: which streets carts can use, who can drive, whether a cart needs a sticker, and whether it can go out after dark. These are our plain-English summaries, each with links to where the rules come from.

Each page shows the day its rules were checked · Always follow local signs

SlowGo routes on roads posted 35 mph or under by default; it doesn’t establish cart access.

Check local rules before you ride. SlowGo doesn’t know each town’s own cart rules yet. Each page below says what it doesn’t apply.

Florida

  • Golf cart laws in FloridaThe state rules every Florida town and county builds on.
  • Apollo BeachOnly county roads the county has designated and signed; we haven’t verified public-road authorization here.
  • ClermontA pilot program: city roads west of US 27 and north of SR 50. $20 registration.
  • Cutler BayAll town streets except US-1, the Turnpike and a short list of busy stretches. One-time $60 permit.
  • DunedinA cart zone of city streets posted 30 mph or less. No city sticker for 20 mph carts.
  • Flagler BeachCity streets, but not A1A or SR 100. Yearly registration and inspection; driver’s license.
  • Fort Walton BeachTwo golf cart zones, streets posted 30 mph or less. $25 city decal; driver’s license.
  • Keaton BeachGolf carts throughout the Keaton Beach area, Saw Grass Estates to J. L. Gibson Road.
  • Key BiscayneVillage roads with a village permit; Crandon Blvd only to cross. Driver’s license.
  • Lynn HavenCity streets in designated subdivisions, posted 25 mph or less.
  • Mexico BeachCity streets, sunrise to sunset. $15 yearly sticker; driver’s license.
  • Miami LakesAll town streets except NW 67th and NW 87th Avenues. Yearly inspection; driver’s license.
  • Nassau CountyOnly county roads designated by resolution, in daylight. County registration.
  • North PortOnly signed roads in Holiday Park, Lakeside Plantation and Plantation Blvd, posted 30 mph or less.
  • OldsmarCity streets posted 25 mph or less, except the two industrial parks. Insurance required.
  • OviedoCity roads posted 30 mph or less, minus a list of busy roads. $30 yearly registration.
  • Palmetto BayAll village streets except Old Cutler Rd, US 1 and eight busy avenues and streets. Village permit.
  • Pine IslandLee County roads on the island, minus a few busy stretches. Daylight only; driver’s license.
  • Pinellas County golf cart zonesNine signed golf cart zones in unincorporated areas, including Crystal Beach, Ozona and Palm Harbor. Driver’s license.
  • Port RicheyAll city streets except US 19 and a list of busier roads. Yearly decal, insurance; driver’s license.
  • Port St. JoeCity roads posted 30 mph or less. $10 yearly decal; 16+ with a license.
  • St. CloudA downtown zone between US 192 and Lakeshore Blvd. $25 yearly registration and inspection.
  • St. Johns CountyOnly designated county roads posted 25 mph or less, and designated multi-use paths.
  • St. Pete BeachDesignated city streets only; no state or county roads, not even to cross. City decal; licensed drivers.
  • SteinhatcheeEvery county-maintained road posted 25 mph or less.
  • TequestaDesignated, signed village streets in the Country Club area. Yearly police registration.
  • Winter GardenOnly golf cart communities and streets approved by resolution, including the Downtown Golf Cart District.

Georgia

  • Golf cart laws in GeorgiaThe state rules: carts are PTVs, and towns decide which streets they can use.
  • Jekyll IslandQualifying PTVs on specified public streets (not state highways or paths). 16+ with a license.
  • Peachtree City100+ miles of shared-use paths. City decal; non-residents pay a path fee.
  • Port WentworthCity roads posted 25 mph or less, minus a list of busy roads. Yearly police registration; 16+ licensed.
  • RomeDesignated 25 mph zones only. Police inspection and registration; 18+.
  • St. Simons IslandPublic streets posted 25 mph or less. County decal; 16+ with a license.
  • Tybee IslandCity streets except Highway 80, which carts cross only at designated locations. Sticker; 16+ licensed.
  • WoodstockRegistered PTVs on city streets posted 25 mph or less. $15 decal; licensed drivers.

Arizona

  • Golf cart laws in ArizonaNo statewide cart road law: road carts need MVD registration. NEVs (low-speed vehicles) can’t travel along roads posted over 35.
  • MaranaRoad carts need MVD registration, insurance and a licensed driver. Not on sidewalks or paths.

Follow the law and posted signs. Follow applicable law, posted restrictions and lawful directions from officers. If permission is unclear, don’t use that road or path until confirmed. Rules can change after we check them.

Town staff: see something out of date? Email hello@slowgoapp.com and we’ll fix it quickly.
