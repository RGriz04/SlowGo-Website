Local cart rules

Golf carts in Captiva Island

SlowGo is an independent app, not affiliated with Lee County. For official rules, see Lee County Ordinance 09-22 (PDF).

On Captiva, golf carts can use the county roads that Lee County DOT has designated and signed for them, in daylight, with a licensed driver.

Where carts can go

County roads on Captiva that the Lee County Department of Transportation has designated for golf carts. The ordinance doesn’t list the roads; the county signs the ones that are open to carts. If you don’t see a sign, ask Lee County DOT.

Who can drive

Only people with a valid driver’s license from their home state or country. Florida’s late-night limits for young licensed drivers also apply to carts.

Cart and equipment

The cart can’t be able to go faster than 20 mph. It needs good brakes, reliable steering, safe tires, a rearview mirror, and red reflectors front and rear. The county ordinance has no registration or sticker.

After dark

Sunrise to sunset, unless Lee County DOT has designated a road for night use. Then the cart also needs headlights, brake lights, turn signals and a windshield.

Low-speed vehicles

A low-speed vehicle (LSV) is different from a golf cart: it goes faster than 20 mph (up to 25), and it has to be titled, registered and insured, with a licensed driver. Under Florida law an LSV can use streets posted 35 mph or less and can cross faster roads at intersections, unless the local government has closed a road to them.

Always follow local signs. Posted signs and local officers have the final word, and rules can change after we check them. If a sign says something different from this page, follow the sign.

Official sources

  • Lee County Ordinance 09-22 (2009), golf carts on designated county roads on Captiva Island — Lee County (PDF)
  • Florida Statutes § 316.212 — golf carts on roads
  • Florida Statutes § 316.2122 — low-speed vehicles

Using SlowGo here

SlowGo plans routes on roads posted 35 mph and under. It doesn’t know which Captiva roads Lee County has signed for carts, so follow the signs over the route.
