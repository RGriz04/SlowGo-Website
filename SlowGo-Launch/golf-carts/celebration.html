Osceola County publishes the golf cart rules that cover Celebration. Here’s a plain-English summary.

Local cart rules · Updated October 2026

Golf cart rules in Celebration, Florida

Golf carts aren’t allowed on Osceola County roads in Celebration: the county keeps carts off its roads unless it passes an ordinance for a community, and we found none for Celebration. Low-speed vehicles (LSVs) can use streets posted 35 mph or less under Florida law.

Do you need a driver’s license to drive a golf cart in Celebration?

Golf carts aren’t allowed on Celebration’s county roads, as far as we found. An LSV driver needs a valid driver’s license.

How old do you have to be to drive a golf cart in Celebration?

The county’s policy doesn’t set an age for Celebration, since carts aren’t allowed there. Where carts are allowed in Florida, drivers under 18 need a learner’s permit or a driver’s license, and drivers 18 and over need a government-issued photo ID.

Does a golf cart need a permit or registration in Celebration?

There’s no cart permit for Celebration. A community can ask the county to open its roads: a homeowner or HOA application, a petition signed by 51% of owners, a county traffic study (the fee can be up to $6,500), and a county ordinance, with the applicant paying for the signs.

Where can golf carts go in Celebration?

Not on Osceola County roads or sidewalks. The county prohibits golf carts on or along its roads and sidewalks unless an engineering study supports it and the county board adopts an ordinance for that community. The county has done that for Harmony and Pine Grove; we found no such ordinance for Celebration.

Some Celebration streets may belong to a community district or be private. Their owners set the rules there.

Golf cart or LSV: what’s the difference in Celebration?

A low-speed vehicle (LSV) is different from a golf cart: it goes faster than 20 mph (up to 25), and it has to be titled, registered and insured, with a licensed driver. Under Florida law an LSV can use streets posted 35 mph or less and can cross faster roads at intersections, unless the local government has closed a road to them.

Can you drive a golf cart at night in Celebration? Hours and lights

For golf carts, the question doesn’t come up on county roads here. LSVs follow Florida law.

Always follow local signs. Posted signs and local officers have the final word, and rules can change after we check them. If a sign says something different from this page, follow the sign.

Using SlowGo here

SlowGo plans routes on roads posted 35 mph and under. It doesn’t know that golf carts are barred from Celebration’s county roads, so it will still plan a route for a golf cart here. Don’t take that route in a golf cart; an LSV can use it.
