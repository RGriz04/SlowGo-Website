The City of Tybee Island publishes its golf cart rules. Here’s a plain-English summary.

Local cart rules · Updated October 2026

Golf cart rules in Tybee Island, Georgia

Tybee lets registered, inspected carts use every city street except Highway 80, which carts may only cross at four places. Drivers must be licensed and 16 or older.

Rules checked October 2026 · Always follow local signs

Do you need a driver’s license to drive a golf cart in Tybee Island?

Yes. Carts are for licensed drivers.

How old do you have to be to drive a golf cart in Tybee Island?

Licensed drivers 16 or older.

Does a golf cart need a permit or registration in Tybee Island?

Yes. Every cart is inspected each year and carries a T-number sticker on the bottom left of the windshield. The sticker is a one-time $15; renewals are free. Both are done at Parking Services, 1509 Strand Avenue (call ahead: officers are sometimes out). Tybee residents bring a vehicle registration showing Chatham County District 3.

Insurance must meet the state minimum for cars ($25,000 per person, $50,000 per accident, $15,000 property). Homeowner’s policies aren’t accepted. Keep proof in the cart.

Required equipment: headlights, tail lights, brake lights, turn signals, a top-mounted flashing amber light, a horn and seat belts. The cart can’t be able to go faster than 20 mph.

Where can golf carts go in Tybee Island?

All city streets except Highway 80. Carts may cross Highway 80 only at:

  • Highway 80/Butler Avenue, at locations south of the Park of 7 Flags
  • the traffic light at 14th Street
  • the traffic light at Campbell Avenue
  • the traffic light at Jones Avenue

No carts on sidewalks.

Golf cart or LSV: what’s the difference in Tybee Island?

Tybee’s cart rules are written for carts that can’t go faster than 20 mph. Under Georgia law, a low-speed vehicle (LSV) is a separate class: a four-wheeled electric vehicle with a top speed over 20 mph and not over 25 mph. We didn’t find LSV rules on the city’s cart page, so ask the city before you drive an LSV here.

Can you drive a golf cart at night in Tybee Island? Hours and lights

We didn’t find cart hours in the city’s rules. Every cart must have headlights, tail lights, brake lights, turn signals and a top-mounted flashing amber light.

Parking

Cart parking is paid like any other: meter, the ParkTYB app or a decal. Rental carts may already have a pass.

Fines

The city’s flyer says violations can bring fines up to $1,000 or up to 12 months in jail.

Always follow local signs. Posted signs and local officers have the final word, and rules can change after we check them. If a sign says something different from this page, follow the sign.
