Local cart rules · Updated October 2026

Golf cart rules in Gulf Breeze, Florida

Gulf Breeze lets registered golf carts use its quiet neighborhood roads and shared-use paths, including The Loop. Low-speed vehicles get the roads but not The Loop.

Rules checked October 2026 · Always follow local signs

Do you need a driver’s license to drive a golf cart in Gulf Breeze?

The city’s page says drivers 15 and up need at least a valid learner’s permit.

How old do you have to be to drive a golf cart in Gulf Breeze?

The city’s page says no one under 15 may drive a cart.

Does a golf cart need a permit or registration in Gulf Breeze?

Yes. Register the cart with the city (Community Services at City Hall) and put the decal on the windshield. An expired registration can get you a ticket.

Where can golf carts go in Gulf Breeze?

Designated public roads and shared-use paths posted under 25 mph, which the city describes as essentially its residential neighborhoods, and The Loop, the city’s shared-use path for golf carts and other recreational wheels.

Not on the Bob Sikes Bridge or the Pensacola Bay Bridge. Carts can’t cross Gulf Breeze Parkway (US 98) at this time. Never on sidewalks.

Golf cart or LSV: what’s the difference in Gulf Breeze?

A cart modified to go faster than 20 mph becomes a low-speed vehicle: it needs a driver’s license, a state tag and insurance, and it can no longer use the shared-use paths. LSVs can use roads posted 35 mph or less, but not The Loop.

Can you drive a golf cart at night in Gulf Breeze? Hours and lights

After sunset a cart needs headlights, brake lights, turn signals and a windshield. Without them, night driving isn’t legal.

Always follow local signs. Posted signs and local officers have the final word, and rules can change after we check them. If a sign says something different from this page, follow the sign.

Using SlowGo here

SlowGo plans routes on roads posted 35 mph and under. It doesn’t know Gulf Breeze’s under-25 rule yet, so a route can include a street that carts can’t use here. Check the city’s page and map before you ride.
