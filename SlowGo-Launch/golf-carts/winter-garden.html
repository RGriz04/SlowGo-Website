The City of Winter Garden publishes its golf cart rules. Here’s a plain-English summary.

Local cart rules · Updated October 2026

Golf cart rules in Winter Garden, Florida

In Winter Garden, golf carts may use only the communities and streets the city has approved by resolution, such as the Downtown Golf Cart District. Everywhere else, public streets are closed to carts.

Rules checked October 2026 · Always follow local signs

Do you need a driver’s license to drive a golf cart in Winter Garden?

The city’s ordinance lets drivers without a license use a cart only if they’re 18 or older and carry proof of a city-approved safety course. Florida law also applies: drivers under 18 need a learner’s permit or a driver’s license.

How old do you have to be to drive a golf cart in Winter Garden?

Drivers without a license must be at least 18. Under Florida law, drivers under 18 need a learner’s permit or a driver’s license.

Does a golf cart need a permit or registration in Winter Garden?

We didn’t find a registration requirement in what we read. Carts need Florida’s standard equipment: good brakes, reliable steering, safe tires, a rearview mirror, and red reflectors front and rear.

Where can golf carts go in Winter Garden?

Only golf cart communities and streets approved by city resolution, such as the Downtown Golf Cart District. Under the ordinance:

  • no roads posted over 25 mph, unless the resolution allows it
  • a cart may cross a street posted over 25 and up to 35 mph only at a four-way stop or a traffic signal, to keep going along a 25 mph street
  • no city arterial or collector roads, unless the resolution allows it
  • no state highways

The district may have changed since those resolutions. We haven’t read the current district resolution or map, and a resolution can allow a higher speed limit than 25, so check the city’s current district map.

Golf cart or LSV: what’s the difference in Winter Garden?

A low-speed vehicle (LSV) is different from a golf cart: it goes faster than 20 mph (up to 25), and it has to be titled, registered and insured, with a licensed driver. Under Florida law an LSV can use streets posted 35 mph or less and can cross faster roads at intersections, unless the local government has closed a road to them.

Can you drive a golf cart at night in Winter Garden? Hours and lights

Sunrise to sunset. Where the resolution allows night use, carts may run from 5:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. if they have headlights, brake lights, turn signals, a windshield, and side reflectors or reflective tape.

Communities

The city commission can take back a community’s or a street’s approval. Homeowners’ associations enforce their own rules.

Always follow local signs. Posted signs and local officers have the final word, and rules can change after we check them. If a sign says something different from this page, follow the sign.
