The Jekyll Island Authority publishes its rules for golf carts and PTVs. Here’s a plain-English summary.

Local cart rules · Updated October 2026

Golf cart rules in Jekyll Island, Georgia

Jekyll Island is a little different. The street-legal carts most people drive count as personal transportation vehicles (PTVs) under Georgia law, and those are welcome on the island’s streets. True golf carts, the kind built only for the golf course, are not.

Rules checked October 2026 · Always follow local signs

Do you need a driver’s license to drive a golf cart in Jekyll Island?

Yes. PTV drivers need a valid driver’s license.

How old do you have to be to drive a golf cart in Jekyll Island?

At least 16, with a valid driver’s license.

Does a golf cart need a permit or registration in Jekyll Island?

No Glynn County registration is needed on Jekyll. The owner needs liability insurance at Georgia’s minimum limits, with proof kept in the PTV.

Required: brakes and a parking brake, a reverse warning, a main power switch, headlights and taillights, front and rear turn signals, brake lights, reflectors, a rearview mirror, a horn, hip restraints, hand holds, a seat belt for every seat, and the orange slow-moving-vehicle triangle on the back.

Where can golf carts go in Jekyll Island?

PTVs can use the island’s public streets, but not roads that are part of the state highway system. No PTVs on sidewalks, bike paths, the pedestrian-only paths in the Historic District, unimproved trails, service roads or golf-cart paths.

Park in regular vehicle spaces.

Golf cart or LSV: what’s the difference in Jekyll Island?

Georgia law draws the line by speed. A PTV has at least four wheels, a top speed under 20 mph, weighs no more than 1,375 lb empty and carries up to eight people. A golf cart is built only for golf and averages under 15 mph.

On Jekyll, golf carts are not allowed on public streets or Authority property, except to cross a street while playing golf, or at a parade, festival or other event with the sponsor’s OK. Privately owned carts aren’t allowed on the Authority’s golf courses.

LSVs (low-speed vehicles) are registered and insured with the state, can use streets posted 35 mph or less, and are kept off the same paths as PTVs.

Can you drive a golf cart at night in Jekyll Island? Hours and lights

Yes, with lights: PTV lights go on at night, in rain, or when you can’t see 500 feet ahead.

On the road

Everyone wears a seat belt, one person per belt. Children under 8 ride in a child seat (a seat belt is fine if they weigh at least 40 lb or are over 4′9″). If two or more vehicles are behind you, pull over at the first safe spot. No passing in the same lane, and no riding two abreast.

Always follow local signs. Posted signs and local officers have the final word, and rules can change after we check them. If a sign says something different from this page, follow the sign.
