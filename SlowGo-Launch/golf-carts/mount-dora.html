The City of Mount Dora publishes its golf cart rules. Here’s a plain-English summary.

Local cart rules · Updated October 2026

Golf cart rules in Mount Dora, Florida

In Mount Dora, a permitted golf cart can use every city street except a short list of busier roads, around the clock. Carts need a city permit, and drivers under 18 need a learner’s permit or license.

Rules checked October 2026 · Always follow local signs

See Mount Dora’s official golf cart map.

Do you need a driver’s license to drive a golf cart in Mount Dora?

Not at 18 and over: every driver carries a government-issued photo ID, and drivers under 18 need a learner’s permit or a driver’s license.

How old do you have to be to drive a golf cart in Mount Dora?

Drivers must be 18, unless they have a learner’s permit or driver’s license.

Does a golf cart need a permit or registration in Mount Dora?

Yes. A city golf cart permit is required: $25 to register, $10 a year to renew. Bring the application, the affidavit, proof of insurance and a copy of your license to the Police Department (or email them). The sticker goes on the lower driver’s side bumper or fender. Country Club of Mount Dora and private or gated communities inside the city don’t need it.

Required equipment: windshield; driver’s side mirror plus an inside or passenger-side mirror; headlights and tail lights; brakes and a parking brake; front and rear turn signals; steering; rear and side reflectors; a horn; and grab bars on rear-facing seats.

Where can golf carts go in Mount Dora?

All city streets, except:

  • all county roads and state highways, and streets outside city limits
  • US 441, SR 44, SR 46, CR 19A, Old Highway 441, Old Eustis Road, Eudora Road, Round Lake Road, Limit Avenue, Wolf Branch Road, Robie Avenue and Britt Road
  • 5th Avenue from Highland Avenue to Old Highway 441; Highland Avenue from 5th Avenue to the Orange County line; 1st Avenue from US 441 to Highland Avenue; Donnelly Street from US 441 to Limit Avenue

Carts may cross county roads at intersections. They may not cross US 441. No bike paths or sidewalks. Private streets and gated communities set their own rules.

Golf cart or LSV: what’s the difference in Mount Dora?

A cart modified to go faster than 20 mph counts as a low-speed vehicle. It must be titled, registered and insured with the state, and it can’t use sidewalks or streets posted over 35 mph.

Can you drive a golf cart at night in Mount Dora? Hours and lights

24 hours a day, except when the city closes a street for an event. Every permitted cart has headlights and tail lights (see the equipment list).

Passengers

Passengers ride seated, no more than the cart is built for.

Always follow local signs. Posted signs and local officers have the final word, and rules can change after we check them. If a sign says something different from this page, follow the sign.
