Local cart rules

Golf carts in St. Simons Island

On St. Simons, and everywhere else in unincorporated Glynn County, a registered cart (a PTV under Georgia law) can use public streets posted 25 mph or less.

Where carts can go

Public streets posted 25 mph or less. The county publishes a map of St. Simons roads by speed limit. No carts on sidewalks or bike paths, at any time.

Who can drive

At least 16, with a valid driver’s license.

Registration

Register the cart with Glynn County before driving it on public streets: $15, good for five years or until the cart changes hands. The county decal goes on the driver’s side, where it can always be seen.

On the road

One person per seat, and everyone wears a seat belt. Children under 8 ride in a child seat (a seat belt is fine if they weigh at least 40 lb or are over 4′9″). Headlights and taillights on when it isn’t daylight. All the usual traffic laws apply.

Low-speed vehicles

A low-speed vehicle (top speed over 20, up to 25 mph) is registered with the Glynn County Tax Commissioner like any other vehicle, can use streets posted 35 mph or less, and isn’t allowed on sidewalks or bike paths.

Check local signs. Posted signs and local officers have the final word, and rules can change after we check them. If a sign says something different from this page, follow the sign.

Official sources

  • Low-Speed Vehicles (LSV) and Personal Transportation Vehicles (PTV) — Glynn County
  • St. Simons Island roads by speed limit (June 2025) — Glynn County (PDF)
  • Georgia HB 877 (2014), the enacted bill that set the state’s cart (PTV) rules — Georgia General Assembly
  • Georgia Code: low-speed vehicles — Georgia Department of Public Safety
