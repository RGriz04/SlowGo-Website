The City of North Port publishes its golf cart rules. Here’s a plain-English summary.

Local cart rules · Updated October 2026

Golf cart rules in North Port, Florida

In North Port, golf carts can use only the signed roads the city has designated: the city roads in the Holiday Park Recreational District and Lakeside Plantation, plus Plantation Boulevard, all posted 30 mph or less. There’s no city registration for carts.

Rules checked October 15, 2026 · Always follow local signs

State law: see golf cart laws in Florida.

Do you need a driver’s license to drive a golf cart in North Port?

Not always. The city follows Florida law: drivers under 18 need a learner’s permit or a driver’s license, and drivers 18 and over need a government-issued photo ID.

How old do you have to be to drive a golf cart in North Port?

Florida law: drivers under 18 need a learner’s permit or a driver’s license, and drivers 18 and over need a government-issued photo ID.

Does a golf cart need a permit or registration in North Port?

No. The police department’s page says golf carts don’t need a title, registration or insurance.

Where can golf carts go in North Port?

Only the designated, signed roads, all posted 30 mph or less:

  • all city roads in the Holiday Park Recreational District
  • all city roads in the Lakeside Plantation community
  • Plantation Boulevard

A cart may cross a county road where it meets an approved cart road, a golf course or a mobile home park.

Golf cart or LSV: what’s the difference in North Port?

A low-speed vehicle (LSV) is different from a golf cart: it goes faster than 20 mph (up to 25), and it must be titled, registered and insured, with a licensed driver. LSVs can use roads posted 35 mph or less, but North Port bans them, whatever the speed limit, on Atwater Drive, Biscayne Drive, Chamberlain Boulevard, Cranberry Boulevard, Haberland Boulevard, Pan American Boulevard, Panacea Boulevard, Price Boulevard, Salford Boulevard, San Mateo Drive, Sumter Boulevard and Toledo Blade Boulevard. An LSV can’t travel along roads posted over 35; lawful crossings are separately regulated.

Can you drive a golf cart at night in North Port? Hours and lights

We didn’t find a local hours rule. Under Florida law, golf carts may use roads only from sunrise to sunset, unless the local government allows night use and the cart has headlights, brake lights, turn signals and a windshield.

Follow the law and posted signs. Follow applicable law, posted restrictions and lawful directions from officers. If permission is unclear, don’t use that road or path until confirmed. Rules can change after we check them.
