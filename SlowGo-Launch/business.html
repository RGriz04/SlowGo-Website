For businesses

SlowGo for businesses

SlowGo is navigation for golf carts and low-speed vehicles. It’s free for riders, with no ads and no account. If your business puts people in carts, or welcomes people who ride them, here’s how we can work together.

Pick the one that fits you. Each has a short form, and Ron reads every one himself.

  • Rental shops

    Free cards with your shop’s name and its own QR code, and a daily count of how often your link is opened.

    Get in touch →

  • Dealers

    Free “Your cart from [your dealership]” cards with your own QR code, for every cart you sell.

    Get in touch →

  • Communities & HOAs

    We check how SlowGo routes around your community, and send cards or a QR code for residents.

    Get in touch →

  • Towns & tourism

    Tell us about streets carts can’t use, new paths or closures. Our cart rules pages link to your published rules.

    Get in touch →

  • Realtors & partners

    Cards with a QR code for closing gifts, welcome bags and open houses, and ideas for working together.

    Get in touch →
