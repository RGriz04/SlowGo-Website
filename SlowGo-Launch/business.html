SlowGo for businesses

SlowGo is navigation for golf carts and low-speed vehicles. It’s free for riders, with no ads and no account. If your business puts people in carts, or welcomes people who ride them, here’s how we can work together.

  • Rental shops
  • Dealers
  • Communities & HOAs
  • Towns & tourism
  • Realtors & partners

Cart rental shops

Give every renter a better first ride, and see how many of your cards get used.

  • Free SlowGo cards with your shop’s name and its own QR code (slowgoapp.com/f/your-shop).
  • A count of how many times your cards are scanned, by day. Never who scanned them or where anyone rode.
  • Coming next, in testing now and not in the App Store yet: your name and logo in the app, “Return your cart here” saved for the renter, a Call the shop button, and your own recommended spots.

Please don’t include renter personal information or payment details.

Hand every new owner a way to ride their town on the right roads, with your name on it.

  • Free “Your cart from [your dealership]” cards with your own QR code, for every cart you sell.
  • A count of how many times your cards are scanned, by day. Nothing about the riders.
  • Coming next, in testing now: your name in the app and a “Call for service” button that rings your service desk.

Towns and tourism boards

Point visitors with carts toward the streets you want them on.

  • Our plain-English cart rules pages link to your own published rules, and we correct them when you tell us something changed.
  • Tell us about streets carts can’t use, new paths, or seasonal closures.
  • SlowGo is independent: it isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by any town, and it never presents itself as official.
